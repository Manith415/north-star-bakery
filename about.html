Our Story

North Star Bakery began with a love of baking for friends and neighbors. We want our shop to be a welcoming place to find handmade food and catch up with the community.

We choose ingredients for their freshness and quality. We work with nearby suppliers when possible and use seasonal ingredients in some of our treats.
